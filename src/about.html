We are a dynamic group of friends based in Enschede, actively involved within the study association Inter-Actief and the broader university community. What began as casual hangouts and shared interests quickly blossomed into a tight-knit community of friends eager to organize events, explore new ideas, and support each other academically and socially. Our love for technology, student life, and making memories binds us together — and we extend this warmth to everyone we meet.

To support our growing community, we've developed plans for student events, event management systems, web apps, and membership onboarding tools as S.G.V. Tegel. Through teamwork and creativity, we build a welcoming environment where everyone feels valued, engaged, and part of the greater Inter-Actief community.

Tegel — good times, great people, and plenty of memories in the making.
